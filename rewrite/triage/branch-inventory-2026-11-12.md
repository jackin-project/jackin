## Class definitions

- **VALUABLE** — a feature, fix, or improvement whose content is **not** in `main`. The work still carries value and must not be lost. It is not proven contained.
- **CONTAINED** — the branch's content is **fully in `main`**, and the proof is recorded (merged PR / patch-id equality / ancestry / empty diff against main). Nothing is lost by retiring the branch.
- **DEBRIS** — logs, progress output, temporary scratch work, stash-recovery snapshots, generated artifacts, or duplicates of other branches. No unique product value.
- **VALUABLE (port candidate)** — a sub-class of VALUABLE for branches whose content is absent from `main` and therefore not landable as-is, but whose unique content may still be worth porting onto `main` by hand. Such branches are **excluded from the deletion list** and are never deleted without an explicit operator decision. **0 branches are in this state as of 2026-11-13**: the 3 branches that entered it on 2026-11-13 (`recovery/pr1230-blob-auth-77b399f1-20261027`, `recovery/stash-adoption-6a0d2f03-20261025`, `recovery/strip-tests-restore-20261030`) left it again the same day, when the **completed** portability assessment returned **DISCARD** for all three. They are recorded as **DEBRIS (superseded)** with their port reference kept — see 'Portability assessment - COMPLETED (2026-11-13)'.
- **CONTAINED candidate - UNPROVEN** — the branch is *believed* to be contained, but the proof is incomplete: the paths it changes are ABSENT from `main` and carry product code, so containment is NOT proven. Such branches are counted in the CONTAINED column for tracking only; they must be treated as VALUABLE (NOT deletable) until every absent path is proven to be scratch/archive. 5 branches were in this state, all in `jackin-project/jackin`; their verdicts are now known (2026-11-13): **exactly 4 are CONTAINED-safe-to-delete** (`codex/openshell-integration-checkpoint-20261108-122311`, `codex/openshell-verified-checkpoint-20261108`, `preserve/session-boundaries-20261109`, `preserve/w1-fixes-20261109`) and **1 (`preserve/p0.6-prototype-20261109`) is DEBRIS, not CONTAINED**, so it needs **its own ratification word**.
