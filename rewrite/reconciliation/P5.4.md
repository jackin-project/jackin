## Deviations from plan v2

- Dockerfile placeholder. A bare `FROM scratch AS docker-client-absent` made BuildKit reuse the cached client `COPY` in an absent build (live, `forbidden tool in role image: /usr/local/bin/docker`; the scan failed the build, so the failure was closed). The absent stage now copies a one-file source from `docker-client-none`, and both layers write `/usr/local/lib/jackin/docker-layer`. A test pins that the absent stage is not a bare scratch.
- `dockerd` was not on the forbidden-tool list, so a role image that held it passed the scan (live L3d). Closed on the coordinator's decision: the construct loop and the scan list now hold 19 names (added `dockerd`, `containerd-shim`, `containerd-shim-runc-v2`, `crun`, `buildkitd`, `rootlesskit`). Row `scan_refuses_forbidden_tools_by_name_anywhere_in_the_tree` pins the eight engine names and the count; red on the old list: `left: [containerd, doas, docker, gosu, ...]` (13 names) against the construct's 19 (`scan list against construct`), and `scan_refuses_every_other_tool_...` red at the per-tool row. I6 (f) now plants all eight engine names plus `podman` and `nerdctl` at `/usr/local/bin` with the trusted bytes; the exemption still covers only the three client paths.
- I6 (b) and (c) assert more lines than the review: with the exemption also comparing bytes, a changed `docker` gives the `forbidden tool` and the `differs` line; a symlinked `docker` gives those two and the `protected path is not a real root-owned path (symlink)` line.
- J2 is reduced to the negative at the binary (a workspace flag alone does not change `jackin build`). The fake image engine records no build args, so the positive half is I2 and P15.
- P14 compares the role-only and workspace-only plans through `ImageRequest.docker`, not through the tag: two fixtures have different commits, so their tags differ for another reason.
- The `STATUS` of D07: the label set gained `jackin.dev/docker` (value `off` or the engine port). The orchestrator amends D07 and the API.md label list (`DESIGN.md` is not edited here).
