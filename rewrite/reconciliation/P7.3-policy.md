# P7.3 policy reconciliation

Fix round F1 (host-pattern closure bypass) and F2 (Landlock mode) of the P7.3 security review. Base: 2d6cf46e on `wt/P7.3-policy`. Test plan: `P7.3-policy-test-plan-v2.md` (the edits of `P7.3-plan-review.md` section 1 applied as written). Decisions: D10 and D12 as amended in 2d6cf46e, and the P7.3 rows of `decisions.tsv`.

## What changed

- `HostPattern::parse` (`crates/jackin-openshell/src/policy.rs`) refuses a host unless every label is `[A-Za-z0-9_-]` and `*`, or is exactly `**`. It then refuses every shape that `host_wildcard_shape_invalid` and `TldWildcard` refuse in `openshell-policy` (a whole `*` or `**`, `*.x` and `**.x` with two labels, a first label with `**` that is not exactly `**`, a later label with `**` or with `*` that is not exactly `*`). The existing `openshell_core` `HostPattern::new` call stays last. The check lives in `parse`, so `parse_authored`, `ClosedPolicy::parse` and the live `scan` (`jackin inspect`) use it. A live host that breaks it is reported as `UnparsableHost`.
- `PolicyDocument::parse_authored` refuses a policy whose `landlock.compatibility` is not `hard_requirement` (`PolicyError::LandlockMode { found }`; a missing section and `landlock: {}` are refused), and a policy whose `read_only` and `read_write` lists are both empty (`PolicyError::NoFilesystemPaths`). The live parse and `with_rule` do not apply either check.
- Nine policy literals flip `best_effort` to `hard_requirement`: `jackin-testkit` (`fixtures.rs`, `gateway.rs`, `tests/gateway/support.rs`), `jackin-session` (`tests/plan/roles.rs`, `examples/export_live.rs`, `examples/upload_project.rs`), `jackin-openshell` (`src/contract.rs`, `tests/gateway/support.rs`), and `jackin` (`tests/cli/role_check.rs`). Each is a one-word change.
- `globset 0.4.21` (default features off) is a `[workspace.dependencies]` entry and a `jackin-openshell` dev-dependency. `openshell-policy` was already a dependency; no change.

## Decisions the brief did not specify

1. The gateway shape lives in `HostPattern::parse`, not in a separate pass. Live `**` and `*.com` therefore come out as `UnparsableHost` (the plan allowed `Overlap` or `UnparsableHost`; one assertion pins it in G1).
2. The Landlock check runs before the filesystem check. A policy with both defects reports `LandlockMode`. Tests use one defect each. The L1 row "only `version: 1`" is dropped because it has two defects; "no `landlock` key" covers the same break.
3. `overlaps` keeps `unwrap_or(true)`. It is unreachable through the public API (the alphabet keeps `Err` out of `host_patterns_overlap`), and deleting it needs an `expect`. D1 asserts `host_patterns_overlap(..).is_ok()` for every compared pair, so a later widening of the alphabet turns it red. No test claims the branch.
4. The old closure test over `BYPASS_FORMS` and `OTHER_HOSTS` moved to `tests/host_dialect.rs`, with `sandbox_matches` (globset) as its oracle. Shared helpers (`rule_doc`, `with_header`, `profile`) live in `tests/common/mod.rs`. The `r2 chatgpt.com 8080` sub-assertion was not carried over: `closure_violations_follow_d10_for_hosts_ports_and_binaries` already pins another port on `api.openai.com`.
5. `a_role_policy_that_overlaps_a_codex_host_is_rejected_before_any_plan` (jackin-session) loses its `**` row; `**` is now a parse refusal. That is the one edit in the session tests beyond the literal flip and the new S1 test.
