This section is for people who work on jackin❯ itself.

- **ADRs** are the architecture decision records. [DESIGN.md](https://github.com/jackin-project/jackin/blob/main/rewrite/DESIGN.md) is the authority where an ADR disagrees with it.
- **Brand identity** gives the mark, colors, and asset rules.

To use jackin, see the **jackin❯** tab.
