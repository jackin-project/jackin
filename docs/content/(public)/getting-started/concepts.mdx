## People and machines

The **operator** is you. You run jackin and you own the gateway.

The **gateway** is the OpenShell service that creates sandboxes and holds credentials. You install, start, and register it. Jackin never manages it.

A **sandbox** is the OpenShell environment where one launch runs Codex. OpenShell enforces the isolation.

## What you configure

A **role** is a repository with three files: a `Dockerfile`, a `jackin.role.toml` manifest, and a `policy.yaml` network policy. Jackin builds one image from each role. See [Create a role](/developing/creating-roles/).

A **workspace** is a saved project. It has a name and a directory. It can turn on the [Docker capability](/guides/docker-capability/) and can bind accounts.

An **account** is a named credential for one provider. A **provider** is an AI coding service, such as Codex. The **agent** is the program that runs in the sandbox. Codex is the only agent.

A **binding** links an account to a workspace or to a role. A **default account** is the account that a provider uses when no binding applies. See [Accounts](/guides/accounts/).

## What happens at a launch

`jackin load` runs one launch. A launch has an ID. If a launch stops, you can run it again with the same ID and it continues from where it stopped.

1. Jackin builds the role image, or takes it from the cache, and delivers it to the gateway.
2. Jackin makes sure the OpenShell provider for your account exists.
3. Jackin creates a sandbox, or finds the one with the launch ID, and waits until it is ready.
4. Jackin writes the Codex login placeholder and configuration into the sandbox.
5. Jackin uploads your project as a working tree and a git bundle.
6. Jackin runs the role hooks.
7. Jackin starts Codex and attaches your terminal.

If you cancel, jackin keeps the sandbox. It reports it as a restore candidate.

## What you do afterwards

To **attach** is to open the Codex terminal of a sandbox. Detaching leaves Codex running. A **shell** is a separate terminal process in the sandbox.

To **export** is to fetch the sandbox branches into your repository as `refs/heads/jackin/<sandbox>/<branch>`. To **purge** is to delete the sandbox.

## Usage

**Usage** is the limit data that a provider reports for an account, such as the 5-hour window and the weekly window. `jackin usage` shows it. Usage runs on your host.
