## Source and execution ledger

The <RepoFile path="docs/content/research/context/techniques/tools/refresh-2026-10-24/providers/README.md">provider evidence bundle</RepoFile> contains source URLs, redirect targets, access timestamps, hashes, status/error records, inspected host pins, normalized rate assumptions, offline scripts/results and prior-heading dispositions. Failed fetches remain attempts, not supporting citations.

Official mutable documentation is evidence of what was documented when retrieved. A pinned source file is evidence about that revision. Neither alone proves the npm/binary package delivered by an installer, the installed host's configuration, a real cache hit, a correct task outcome or a paid receipt. Release/package correspondence for the six core products is tracked in their own dossiers.

Retrieval after the cutoff does not prove the page had identical content at the cutoff. Last-modified metadata is retained where available; unversioned sources have this time-resolution limitation. Pinned host commits used here precede the cutoff. Announced December/January prices are labeled future terms and excluded from current-rate calculations.

## Recheck triggers and ownership

| Claim family | Recheck trigger | Evidence needed before changing a decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price and billing normalization | Model/rate-card change, new endpoint or receipt shape | Official price plus real usage schema; fixture proving disjoint accounting |
| Cache mechanics | New model, TTL option, endpoint or host update | Exact minimum, write/read, scope and lifetime contracts; cold/warm receipt when authorized |
| Native hooks | Host version, plugin API, trust or installer change | Versioned source/package plus model-visible replacement and off-path conformance |
| Compaction / thinking | Beta or model update, history-transform change | Compatible continuation, signature behavior and repeated-compaction fidelity |
| Subscription economics | Plan/credit policy, auth route or enterprise agreement change | Applicable official terms and account-authorized observations |
| Fleet sharing | Tenant/workspace or prefix identity change | Authorized scope and isolation proof; never infer scope from a hit counter |
| Quality and efficacy | New task stratum, transformer, model or policy | Independent acceptance and all-attempt task cost under the validation gates |

Keep a stable canonical URL and record its actual resolved destination. Do not create query-string variants to retrieve an older price or treat cached search snippets as current authority. Separate first-party claims, independent studies, local execution and proposed experiments in every table.

## Unknowns and stale claims

An unknown does not prove a feature is unsafe, ineffective or absent. It means the corresponding decision remains unqualified. Preserve the native path while collecting the missing evidence. A current source that contradicts a prior claim should replace it explicitly; a newly unavailable source should not silently become proof that the product disappeared.

The coverage register maps every prior heading in chapters 06/07/10/11/12/13/19/22/23/28 and records withdrawn numerical claims. Old measurements remain historical evidence only when their original fixture, model and denominator are retained. They are not relabeled as fresh execution.
