## Research question

Which techniques improve complete accepted coding-agent tasks without weakening correctness, reliability or maintainability? Keep the selected agent/model/reasoning effort fixed in the compression comparison. Treat cost, actual quota use, context pressure, latency, local resources and operator time as distinct outcomes.

## Current decision

Preserve native optimizations and deliver useful context discipline automatically: precise searches and reads, edit-over-rewrite, clear concise narration, stable prefixes and complete acceptance evidence. Add qualified upstream filters or retrieval backends behind Jackin-owned configuration, recovery and context-epoch contracts. Default, experimental and off profiles are concrete implementation targets; no production optimizer is activated by this research. [Executive decision](/research/context/techniques/00-executive-summary/).

The old universal savings ceilings, workload shares and riskless adoption multipliers are withdrawn where original data or current assumptions do not support them. Neither promise nor rule out 10× without a workload-specific argument. A payload ratio is not a bill, an invoice is not a subscription quota denominator, and recoverable text is not proof that the model noticed missing evidence.

## Fresh evidence

The refresh includes pinned current six-product implementations; selected source builds and binary/package checks; recovery, malformed-data, worktree and reset probes; historical raw-data recomputations; current provider/host contracts; complete offline representation fixtures; and corrected accounting tests. No paid or subscription model task was run. The [evidence ledger](/research/context/tools/07-evidence-and-claims/) records treatment eligibility and uncertainty; [measurement tools](/research/context/techniques/tools/) retain reproducible artifacts.

Current caching includes model-specific read rates, paid write classes and provider-specific continuation rules. Native tool discovery and concise styles change the appropriate baseline. Multimodal accounting and coordinates depend on exact model and host behavior. Model routing, effort control and full-product personas remain separate experiments. Source availability, permissive licensing and successful compilation each answer different reuse questions.
