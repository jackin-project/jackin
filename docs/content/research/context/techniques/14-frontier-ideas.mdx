**Research state:** Current

Source cutoff: **2026-10-24T01:15:00Z**. Primary papers and current provider interfaces were reopened on October 24. All sixteen earlier proposals remain covered; unsupported workload forecasts are replaced with testable conditions.

## Summary

Several mechanisms are implementable, but none earns a universal daily saving or a quality-neutral label from its design alone. Learned representations require compatible model/serving access; codebooks and pruning change what a model must interpret; warming spends money before reuse is known. Native discovery, compaction, caching and budget controls change the baseline against which a new implementation competes.

The earlier **$17/$22 daily profiles, 20–35% integrated forecast, fixed thinking shares, tokenizer floors and 19-hour keepalive rule are withdrawn as current evidence**. Their underlying sessions were not recovered, provider behavior changed, and several mechanisms targeted overlapping costs. Unknown benefit is not zero benefit. Use the [measurement ledger](/research/context/techniques/01-economics-and-measurement/) and [canonical gates](/research/context/tools/07-evidence-and-claims/).

Keep the user's selected model and effort fixed in the primary comparison. Model routing, fine-tuning, changed reasoning policies and self-hosted inference are separately authorized treatments.

## Question and method

For each mechanism: what changes, where can it run, which observed cost classes can it affect, what can fail, and which small experiment could reject it? Reopened abstracts establish paper identity, reported mechanism and evaluation scope; they do not reproduce training or agent outcomes. The <RepoFile path="docs/content/research/context/techniques/tools/refresh-2026-10-24/evaluation/frontier-source-register.json">source register</RepoFile> and coverage ledger record the earlier proposals and numerical withdrawals.

## Model and serving mechanisms

### K1. Gist, ICAE, AutoCompressors, Activation Beacon and 500xCompressor

These papers compress different objects and require different interfaces. They are not interchangeable evidence for reducing a hosted provider's bill.

| Primary version | Reported mechanism / result | Transfer limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Gist v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08467v3) | Trained reusable gist tokens; up to 26× prompt compression, 4.2% wall-time improvement | LLaMA-7B/FLAN-T5-XXL instruction experiments; ratio differs from speedup |
| [ICAE v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06945v4) | Trained memory slots; 4× compression with about 1% additional parameters | Compatible Llama-based encoder/consumer and training required |
| [AutoCompressors v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14788v2) | Summary vectors over segmented OPT/Llama-2 contexts, including 30,720-token training | Language modeling, demonstrations and retrieval; not exact repository recovery |
| [Activation Beacon v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03462v3) | Layer-key/value compression; reported 8× KV-memory reduction, 2× inference acceleration | Architecture/training and long-context tasks, not hosted billing |
| [500xCompressor v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.03094v1) | Trained adapter, 6×–480× ratios | Retained 62.26–72.89% of its measured uncompressed capability; not quality neutral |

The [survey v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12388v2) separates hard and soft prompt methods. Current [Claude message inputs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/api/messages/create) do not document an arbitrary user-supplied learned-KV/soft-prompt channel. Signed compaction and conversation IDs are different interfaces. This is a public availability boundary, not a law of physics or proof that private provider experiments do not exist.

**Test:** in a separately chosen compatible deployment, compare exact facts, edit constraints and complete tasks at several compression levels. Include encoder/training cost and cold startup. Do not apply a paper ratio to all input/output dollars.

### K2. KV state as a persistent artifact

[LMCache's multiprocess architecture](https://docs.lmcache.ai/mp/index.html) places a cache service alongside compatible inference engines, with storage/transfer and process isolation. [CacheGen v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07240v6) reports 3.5×–4.3× KV-size reduction and faster context loading on its serving experiments. No installation or GPU experiment ran here; the old 0.4.6 label is not retained as the current release identity.

For uniform attention, KV bytes scale approximately with <code>2 × layers × KV_heads × head_dimension × bytes_per_value × retained_tokens</code>, before allocator/transfer overhead. Hybrid attention and quantization change that estimate. Storage rent omits accelerator time, bandwidth, restores, recomputation and maintenance. The earlier S3-versus-hosted-write comparison did not price equivalent services.

**Test:** bind restored state to model weights, tokenizer, positions/layout, engine version, tenant and exact prefix; exercise invalidation and failed restore. Compare complete self-hosted economics. No public hosted-API KV-export contract was established; a cache identifier is not a portable KV artifact.

### K3. Provider context editing plus external memory

Current [context editing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/context-editing), [compaction](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/compaction) and native memory are existing baselines. The historical [context-management announcement](https://claude.com/resources/articles/context-management) reports 84% fewer tokens in a 100-turn web-search experiment and separate internal search improvements of 29% with editing or 39% with editing plus memory. These are different first-party evaluations, not one equal-quality coding result.

Deletion affects cache reuse and can remove facts needed later. Signed-thinking rules constrain external history rewriting; provider editing and an arbitrary proxy summary are not equivalent. Memory adds retrieval, stale-state and instruction-authority questions.

**Test:** long tasks crossing several compactions, delayed rare facts, resume and failed summaries; count iterations, recovery and read/write classes. K3 can compose with K15 at different boundaries. Neither automatic redundancy nor an independent multiplier is justified.

### K4. Distill standing context into a fine-tuned model

Training can replace repeated examples on a stable, high-volume task. It changes the model, may weaken rare requirements and does not provide exact provenance or a reliable mutable-policy store.

At retrieval, the [Claude glossary](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/glossary) says its API does not generally offer fine-tuning; [Bedrock's supported table](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/custom-model-fine-tuning.html) lists Claude 3 Haiku. This is a scoped public-availability observation, not a universal statement about private programs. The old 32K statement and blanket “economically dead” verdict are withdrawn.

**Test:** amortize data curation, training, evaluation, deployment, drift and rollback over accepted tasks against cached prompting. Keep permissions, changing repository facts and authoritative instructions explicit. This is outside the unchanged-model primary experiment.

### K5. Provider cache-price routing

The current [DeepSeek price card](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/) separates cache-hit, cache-miss and output prices. A low hit price motivates investigation; the former 276× comparison did not establish current task quality, reuse or total cost.

**Test:** exact endpoint/model, billing route, every token class, tools, retries, escalation and cache fragmentation on the routed task class. A gateway also owns credentials, streaming and continuation. Compare native routing/advisor features. Another model/provider is an explicit policy change, not a benefit credited to a fixed-model reducer.

## Representation and reasoning mechanisms

### K6. In-context session codebooks

A mapping can replace repeated references with shorter aliases. Benefit depends on legend and lookup instructions, reference frequency, output use and recovery. The current [representation fixtures](/research/context/techniques/05-tokenizer-arbitrage/) show why characters alone cannot establish savings; the old 212-token dictionary and 71% message reduction were not reproduced.

**Test:** immutable versioned dictionaries, collision rejection, exact path/identifier restoration, unknown aliases and reloading after compaction/resume. Keep tool arguments and user-facing references usable. A model-facing alias does not authorize repository renaming or schema changes.

### K7. Single-token semantic anchors

The old universal two-token floor was unjustified. Tokenization depends on the exact string, neighboring text and model; a short alias does not carry independently recoverable meaning. Adding a vocabulary token or learned representation is a different model intervention.

**Test:** count the complete relevant message with the actual estimator or a labeled proxy, then test comprehension. Do not subtract one assumed wrapper constant from unrelated requests. K7 targets the same references as K6 and earns no separate additive saving.

### K8. A persistent abbreviation registry

Registry reuse also creates carrying and synchronization costs. A process remembering a mapping is not proof that the current model or child agent saw it. A global mutable registry can give the same alias different worktree meanings.

**Test:** scope by repository, dictionary version, consumer and context epoch. Measure reuse and reload cost; retain old versions while live contexts reference them. Eviction must not silently invalidate historical aliases. The fixed 1.3-use/day threshold is retired; [memory lifecycle](/research/context/techniques/08-retrieval-memory-and-state/) and observed billing determine any useful threshold.

### K9. Templates and repeated-structure encoding

A template plus parameters can represent a test matrix or configuration more compactly than enumeration. Deterministic expansion can preserve an exact output set; asking a model to infer the expansion introduces another task.

**Test:** compare expanded bytes or typed records, including order, escapes, omissions, duplicates and size limits; include grammar/decoder overhead. Generated executable scripts require normal code checks. A bounded declarative format need not execute arbitrary input. The old 544→74 count is not a current task result. [Programmatic tool calls](/research/context/techniques/12-provider-features/) and native exact edits are existing alternatives.

### K10. Token-aware identifier naming

Readable familiar words may tokenize better than obscure abbreviations, but no casing or vowel-removal rule holds universally. The new fixture ties its camel/snake examples. Language conventions, public APIs, config keys, stack traces and searchability matter.

**Test:** measure the actual eligible identifier share and complete code/response counts. Retain readability and compatibility. Do not bulk-rename for a speculative saving; callers, migrations, cache invalidation and review time count. A linter can report evidence without imposing a new convention.

### K11. Tokenizer-aware model routing

Identical bytes can have different counts and prices across models. The former 13.5× fresh/read/write equivalence is not current: tokenizers, read ratios, tiers and defaults differ. A local encoding is not another provider's receipt.

**Test:** the actual model pair on complete accepted tasks, including delegate setup/output, escalation and cache fragmentation. Native Explore is not universally Haiku today. This remains a separate [routing treatment](/research/context/techniques/10-model-routing-and-delegation/); the primary comparison preserves model and effort.

### K12. Shorter reasoning and effort controls

[Chain of Draft v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18600v2) reports terse intermediate reasoning on particular tasks. Its best-case token fraction does not bound hidden-reasoning cost in another model. Visible style, provider effort and output caps are distinct interventions.

**Test:** reported generation/iteration fields and independent acceptance. Do not infer billed hidden thinking by subtracting estimated visible text from output. Fixed-effort terse narration is a possible style arm; changed effort requires another declared arm. Current [task-budget and effort contracts](/research/context/techniques/12-provider-features/) supersede fixed 45%/54.8% shares and a guessed 50% reduction.

## Operational mechanisms

### K13. Paid cache warming and renewal

Warming can reduce later creation or latency if reuse occurs and the exact prefix remains eligible. Native TTLs depend on billing route; Claude Code now exposes TTL controls, and newer OpenAI write classes have different renewal semantics. Universal one-hour defaults and “no TTL control” are stale. See [cache contracts](/research/context/techniques/07-caching-exploitation/).

An expected-cost condition is <code>reuse_probability × avoided_resume_cost &gt; all_warm_cost + maintenance_cost</code>, using the actual policy. Include unused warms, expiry, failures, rate limits and changed prefixes. A read can refresh one lifetime without restarting a longer clock. There is no universal profitable gap.

**Test:** measure the native path first, then an explicitly enabled warm policy at realistic gaps. Keep API cost separate from opaque plan utilization. Do not resume an agent for a hidden ping: it can generate output, tools and state changes. Supported zero-generation prewarms are also paid and require compatible settings. No daemon or warm request was activated here.

### K14. Context-budget CI checks

A checker can detect growth in genuinely loaded instructions, catalogs and schemas. It cannot establish that deleting required guidance improves quality. File globs miss managed/global/dynamic inputs and may count unloaded files.

**Test:** the pinned host's loading inventory, retained requirements and reproducible baseline counts. [Claude counting](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/token-counting) is a free estimate with separate limits; authenticated source disclosure still needs the normal authorized route. Label offline proxy counts. No authenticated counting ran. Prefer an explained regression report before an arbitrary ceiling; the old 1,500/3,000 budgets and $1/day forecast were not universal evidence.

### K15. Learned text pruning at ingestion

[LLMLingua-2 v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12968v2) learns extractive token classification with a bidirectional encoder. It is not simply the earlier causal-perplexity heuristic. MeetingBank, LongBench, ZeroScrolls, GSM8K and BBH results motivate evaluation, not guarantees for code, logs or exact identifiers.

Transforming a new observation once can preserve earlier history, but needs a real replacement seam, durable original, scope, explicit recovery and bounded inference cost. Prose can carry decisive negation and exceptions; “prose-only” is not a safety proof. Exact patches, machine protocols and authorization evidence need stronger contracts.

**Test:** rare facts, negation and values plus complete tasks, cold loading, CPU/RAM, small-input bypass and recovery amplification. Current [host capabilities](/research/context/techniques/23-cross-agent-portability/) make a blanket “not switchable in Claude Code” claim too broad. No weights or pruning inference ran here.

### K16. Jackin as policy delivery

Jackin can deliver an inspectable versioned policy through its actual configuration paths. That does not expose every feature in every adapter or justify silently changing model, effort, auth, TTL or spend. The [integration review](/research/context/tools/11-jackin-integration/) separates registered adapters, launch dispatch and proposed controls.

**Test:** effective configuration for exact host/version, native defaults, one transform owner, fallback to the already-captured authorized result, explicit root/session/epoch, deadlines and off-path conformance. Preserve native execution, editing and the actual configured permission boundary; permissive launch flags do not create approval prompts. Proposed schema fields need repository migration/validation; this research does not implement a token-policy switch.

Economics are the measured marginal effects of selected components, not the sum of K1–K15. Use proportionate checks for small native improvements and canonical quality, all-attempt cost and latency gates for a new complex runtime. [Composition](/research/context/techniques/15-composed-stacks/) covers interactions.

## Retired folklore and uncertainty

| Earlier claim | Current disposition |
|---|---|
| Fewer characters, vowels or glyphs always save tokens | Count exact bytes with an identified encoder; preserve language and readability |
| Base64/gzip is directly understood compressed context | Requires decoding/tool or trained-model support; encoded size is not billed savings |
| Single-token aliases are physically impossible | Withdrawn; tokenization and shared meaning are separate questions |
| Fine-tuning is always dominated by caching | Withdrawn; availability, volume, training and acceptance determine economics |
| Keepalive reads are free or always preserve long TTL | Withdrawn; policies differ and requests cost money |
| Smaller budgets or pruning automatically improve quality | Withdrawn; retained requirements and acceptance must be checked |
| A policy pack has a known 20–35% benefit | Withdrawn; no current complete-task observation supports the forecast |

No current Jackin workload distribution, paid frontier treatment, GPU deployment or account quota experiment was measured. Next obtain a small falsifying fixture and then [task evidence](/research/context/techniques/16-validation-harness/), rather than another daily multiplier.
